Polypall Robotics
Founder & CEO — Polypall
Polypall is an emerging technology company focused on developing intelligent companion and security robotics for residential and personal use. Our work integrates robotics, security, and human-safety design to create devices that provide comfort, monitoring, and protection without the high cost of a therapy pet or a guard dog.
Our product initiatives include: • Stationary security units that function as autonomous “guard presence” systems, using audio deterrence and live alerts to notify owners of unauthorized approach. • Mobile companion robots designed to support individuals through daily life, self care, and safety threats, particularly for people who live alone or are recovering from trauma. •Integrated wellbeing features, including routine-check assistance (hygiene reminders, safety prompts) and community-support connectivity through an optional app platform. Polypall’s mission is to build robotics that increase both safety and psychological resilience, systems that protect the home and support the person inside it. We are currently in early development and prototyping stages, with a focus on ethical design, accessibility, and real-world usability.
